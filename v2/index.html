Services

Cleaning is only a third of the job.

A pressure washer makes things look good for a month. Cleaning, protecting and maintaining makes them stay that way.

  • Roof cleaning

    Moss scraped and soft-washed off tiles and slates at low pressure — clean, without cracked tiles.

    Clean

  • Driveways & patios

    Block paving, flags, tarmac and decking washed back to their original colour, joints re-sanded.

    Clean

  • Render & cladding

    Green and red algae staining lifted from render, cladding, fascias and soffits with a gentle soft-wash.

    Clean

  • Window cleaning

    Frames, sills and glass cleaned with purified water, so they dry without streaks or spots.

    Clean

  • Moss & weed removal

    Growth cleared from roofs, paths and paving joints, then treated so it's slow to come back.

    Protect

  • Sealing & treatments

    A sealant or biocide finish that repels water, resists stains and stretches the time between cleans.

    Protect

  • Gutter clearing

    Gutters and downpipes vacuumed out from the ground and checked for flow before they overflow.

    Maintain

  • Solar panel cleaning

    Dirt and bird mess rinsed off with soft brushes and pure water, so panels aren't working through grime.

    Maintain

Seasonal care

What to do, and when.

Looking after a property costs less when it's regular. This is how a typical year runs.

  • This season

    Spring

    March – May

    • Roof moss removal and treatment
    • Patio and driveway deep clean after winter
  • This season

    Summer

    June – August

    • Render and cladding soft-wash
    • Sealing paving while it's dry
    • Solar panels at peak output
  • This season

    Autumn

    September – November

    • Gutter clearing once the leaves are down
    • Windows, fascias and soffits
  • This season

    Winter

    December – February

    • Gutter check after storms
    • Treating slippery algae on paths and steps
