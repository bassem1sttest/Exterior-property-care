The approach

Cleaning is a third of the job.

A pressure washer makes things look good for a month. Doing all three makes them stay that way.

01

Clean

Moss, algae, lichen and years of traffic film, lifted with the right pressure for the surface — not just the highest one.

02

Protect

Biocide treatments and sealants that slow the regrowth, so the result lasts seasons instead of weeks.

03

Maintain

Gutters cleared, panels rinsed, a return visit in the diary. Small, regular jobs that stop the big expensive ones.

Services

Point at what needs doing.

Pick a part of the house, or choose from the list. Most visits cover more than one.

SelectedRoof cleaning

  1. Moss scraped and soft-washed off tiles and slates at low pressure, so the roof comes up clean without cracked tiles or stripped coatings.

  2. Growth cleared from roofs, paths and paving joints, then treated so it's slow to come back.

  3. Gutters and downpipes vacuumed out from the ground and checked for flow — before the overflow reaches your brickwork.

  4. Frames, sills and glass cleaned with purified water for a finish that dries without streaks or spots.

  5. Green and red algae staining removed from render, cladding, fascias and soffits with a gentle soft-wash.

  6. Dirt and bird mess rinsed off with soft brushes and pure water, so your panels aren't generating through a film of grime.

  7. Block paving, flags, tarmac and decking pressure-washed back to their original colour, with joints re-sanded where needed.

  8. A sealant or biocide finish after cleaning that repels water, resists stains and stretches the time between cleans.
